## The Siamese

The **Siamese** is the most famous colorpoint cat. Look for:

- a **pale body** with darker **points** - face mask, ears, legs and tail;
- **deep blue, almond-shaped eyes**;
- a **long, slender, tube-shaped body**, a **wedge-shaped head** and large ears;
- a short, fine coat that lies close to the body.

## The Oriental Shorthair

The **Oriental Shorthair** has the **same body and head** as the Siamese, but it comes
in hundreds of **solid colors and patterns** - black, blue, cream, tabby, bicolor and
more - **without points**. Its ears look even bigger, and its eyes are usually
**green**.

**Dark points and blue eyes?** Siamese. **Same shape, but one color or a tabby
pattern?** Oriental.
