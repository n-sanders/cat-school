## The final challenge

This quiz mixes breeds from every unit, with look-alikes as wrong answers. Take your
time and work through the features in order:

1. **Coat** - short, long, curly or hairless? Solid, tabby, spotted, ticked or
   colorpoint?
2. **Face** - flat, wedge-shaped, round, triangular or square?
3. **Ears** - folded, tufted, huge, or set low?
4. **Eyes** - blue, green, copper or gold?
5. **Body** - cobby, slender, rectangular or whippet-like?

The pass mark for this lesson is 75%. After you pass, keep using **Practice** and
**Review** - fresh photos from The Cat API will keep testing you.
