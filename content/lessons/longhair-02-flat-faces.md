## One face, three coats

All three of these breeds have the same **flat face**: a short, snub nose, big round
eyes, small round-tipped ears and a cobby body. The **coat** tells them apart.

- **Persian** - a **very long, flowing coat** with a full ruff, in many colors from
  white to tabby to bicolor.
- **Himalayan** - a Persian with **colorpoints** (darker face, ears, legs and tail) and
  **blue eyes**. It was created by crossing Persians with Siamese, and CFA shows it as
  a color division of the Persian.
- **Exotic Shorthair** - a Persian with a **short, dense, plush coat** that stands out
  from the body. It is often called "the Persian in pajamas".

## Tips

- **Flat face, long coat, colorpoints?** Himalayan.
- **Flat face, long coat, no points?** Persian.
- **Flat face, short plush coat?** Exotic Shorthair. Do not confuse it with the British
  Shorthair, whose face is round but whose nose is not flat.
