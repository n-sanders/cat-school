## Hairless

- **Sphynx** (Canada) - looks **hairless**, though the skin may have a fine, suede-like
  down. Look for **wrinkled skin**, especially on the head and shoulders, **very large
  ears** and big, lemon-shaped eyes.

## Curly

Both Rex breeds come from England and have short, **wavy coats**. A Devon Rex kitten
can look nearly bald, so do not rely on the coat alone.

- **Devon Rex** - a **pixie** face: **huge ears set low** on the sides of the head,
  big eyes and a short muzzle. The coat is soft and **loosely waved**.
- **Cornish Rex** - built like a **whippet**: tall and slender with an **arched back**
  and a **tucked-up waist**. Its head is egg-shaped with a Roman nose, its ears are set
  high, and its coat lies in tight, even **"marcel" waves**.

**Ears low and wide, pixie face?** Devon. **Arched back and greyhound shape?** Cornish.
