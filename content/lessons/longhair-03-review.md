## Review

| Breed | Quick clue |
| --- | --- |
| Maine Coon | Huge, rectangular, square muzzle, tufted ears |
| Norwegian Forest Cat | Triangular head, straight profile |
| Siberian | Rounded everything, barrel body |
| Persian | Flat face, long flowing coat |
| Himalayan | Flat face, long coat, colorpoints, blue eyes |
| Exotic Shorthair | Flat face, short plush coat |

**Check the face first.** A forest cat has a normal-length muzzle; the Persian family
has a flat face with a snub nose.
