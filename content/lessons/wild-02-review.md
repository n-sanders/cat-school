## Review

| Breed | Quick clue |
| --- | --- |
| Bengal | Rosettes or marbling, glitter, leopard-cat ancestry |
| Egyptian Mau | Natural random spots, gooseberry-green eyes, silver/bronze/smoke |
| Ocicat | Big thumbprint spots in rows, all-domestic |
| Abyssinian | Ticked coat, no spots or stripes on the body |

**Spots or stripes?** A silver tabby American Shorthair has **swirls and stripes**. The
spotted breeds have **separate spots**. If the marks join into lines and swirls, it
is a tabby.
