## Three blue cats

From across the room, these three look almost the same: short, blue-gray coats.
Check them in this order.

1. **Eyes.** Green eyes mean **Russian Blue**. Copper or orange eyes mean British
   Shorthair or Chartreux.
2. **Body.** The Russian Blue is **slim and long-legged**. The British Shorthair is
   **short, broad and cobby**. The Chartreux is **robust**, with a broad body on
   comparatively short, fine-boned legs.
3. **Head.** The British Shorthair's head is **very round** with chubby cheeks. The
   Chartreux has a **narrower muzzle** and a gentle "smiling" look. The Russian Blue
   has a **medium wedge** with a blunt muzzle - not the long Siamese wedge - and a
   slight smile too.
4. **Coat.** The Russian Blue's coat has **silver tips** that shimmer. The British
   Shorthair's is **dense and plush**. The Chartreux's is dense and slightly **woolly**.

Remember that British Shorthairs come in many colors - but if a blue cat has copper
eyes and a perfectly round face, it is probably a British Shorthair.
