## Review

Before the quiz, run through the clues for each breed:

| Breed | Quick clue |
| --- | --- |
| American Shorthair | Sturdy working cat, classic silver tabby |
| British Shorthair | Round everything, plush blue coat, copper or gold eyes |
| Scottish Fold | Ears folded forward and down |
| Russian Blue | Slim, shimmering blue coat, green eyes |
| Chartreux | Sturdy, woolly blue coat, copper-orange eyes |
| Bombay | Glossy solid black, copper eyes |
| Burmese | Compact and heavy, satin sable-brown coat |

Three of these breeds are often blue-gray - the British Shorthair, Russian Blue and
Chartreux. You will come back to them in the mastery unit.
