## Five patterns to know

The **pattern** of a coat is often the fastest clue to a breed.

- **Solid** - one color all over, like the black Bombay or the blue Russian Blue.
- **Tabby** - stripes, swirls or blotches on a lighter background. Most tabbies also
  have a dark **"M"** on the forehead.
- **Spotted** - separate dark spots instead of stripes, like the Egyptian Mau or Bengal.
- **Ticked** - each hair has bands of dark and light color. From a distance the coat
  looks like one warm, glowing color with **no spots or stripes** on the body. The
  Abyssinian is the classic ticked cat.
- **Colorpoint** - a pale body with darker **points**: the face mask, ears, legs and
  tail. Colorpoint cats almost always have **blue eyes**. The Siamese is the best-known
  example.

## Watch out

Pattern is a clue, not an answer. Plenty of ordinary random-bred cats are tabbies, and
several different breeds are colorpoint. Always check the coat length, face and body
too.
