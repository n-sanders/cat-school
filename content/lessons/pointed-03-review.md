## Review

| Breed | Coat | Quick clue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Siamese | Short, colorpoint | Slender wedge shape, deep blue eyes |
| Oriental Shorthair | Short, solid or patterned | Siamese shape without points, usually green eyes |
| Birman | Medium-long, colorpoint | White gloves on all four paws |
| Ragdoll | Semi-long, colorpoint | Large; colorpoint, mitted, bicolor, or van |

Start with **coat length** (short or fluffy), then check the **paws and face** for
white.
