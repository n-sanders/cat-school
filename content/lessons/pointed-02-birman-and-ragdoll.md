## Two fluffy colorpoints

Both breeds have **colorpoint** coats, **blue eyes** and **medium-long, silky fur**, so
they look like long-haired Siamese at first glance.

- **Birman** - the "Sacred Cat of Burma". Its signature is **pure white "gloves"** on
  all four paws. The coat is silky and rarely mats, with a heavy ruff around the neck.
- **Ragdoll** - a **large, heavy** cat. The name comes from the breeder's claim that
  the foundation cat went limp when held. Most Ragdolls today do not do that.
  Ragdolls come in four styles:
  - *colorpoint* - points with no white;
  - *mitted* - white paws, a white chin, and a white stripe down the belly;
  - *bicolor* - lots of white, including an upside-down white "V" on the face;
  - *van* - mostly white, with color kept to the ears, tail, and mask.

## Tips

- **Neat white gloves on all four paws, nothing else white?** Most likely a Birman.
- **A big cat with a white "V" on the face, a white chin and belly stripe, or color only on the ears, tail, and mask?** Ragdoll.
- **Short, close-lying coat?** That is a Siamese, not either of these.
