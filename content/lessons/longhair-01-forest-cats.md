## Built for the cold

These three breeds all have **thick, shaggy, water-resistant coats**, a full **ruff**
on the chest and a long, **bushy tail**. They developed naturally in cold places, so
at first they look very alike. The **head** is the best clue.

- **Maine Coon** (United States) - one of the **largest** domestic breeds, with a
  long, **rectangular** body. Look for big ears with **lynx-like tufts** at the tips,
  a **square muzzle**, and a coat that is shorter on the shoulders and longer on the
  belly.
- **Norwegian Forest Cat** (Norway) - its head is an **equal-sided triangle**, and the
  **profile is straight**: the nose runs in one line from the brow to the tip. Its back
  legs are longer than its front legs, so the rump sits clearly higher than the
  shoulders. Do not use the rump alone. A Siberian can show a slighter rise.
- **Siberian** (Russia) - built from **circles**: a rounded head, rounded muzzle, round
  eyes and a **barrel-shaped**, very muscular body. Its hind legs are slightly longer
  than its front legs, and its back arches a little higher than the shoulders.

## Tips

- **Square muzzle and tufted ears?** Maine Coon.
- **Straight-line profile and triangular head?** Norwegian Forest Cat.
- **Everything round?** Siberian.
