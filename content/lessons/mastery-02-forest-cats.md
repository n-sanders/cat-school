## Shape, not fur

All three forest cats are big and shaggy, often in brown tabby. Ignore the fur and look
at the **shapes**.

| | Maine Coon | Norwegian Forest Cat | Siberian |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Head | Slightly longer than wide | Equal-sided triangle | Rounded |
| Profile | Gentle curve | Straight line, brow to nose | Gentle curve |
| Muzzle | Square | Straight, follows the triangle | Rounded |
| Body | Long and rectangular | Rump clearly higher than the shoulders | Barrel-shaped, with a slight rise at the rump |

**Quick test:** imagine drawing the head. If you would draw a **box**, it is a Maine
Coon. A **triangle**: Norwegian Forest Cat. A **circle**: Siberian.

A higher rump is not the Norwegian's alone. The Siberian also has slightly longer
hind legs, and its back sits a little higher than its shoulders. Use the head.
