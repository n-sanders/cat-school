## Round shapes

Both of these shapes are **curved all the way around**, with no corners or straight
edges.

- A **circle** is equally round everywhere. Every point on its edge is the same
  distance from the center.
- An **ellipse** (an oval) looks like a circle that has been **stretched** in one
  direction.

**Circle or ellipse?** Compare the width and the height. If they are the same, it is a
circle.
